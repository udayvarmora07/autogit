# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and
focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks,
and deliberately unsafe behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the GitHub repository security
advisory channel or directly to the repository maintainer. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially when practical. Maintainers may remove comments,
close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project
safe and welcoming.
